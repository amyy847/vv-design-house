# Project descriptions

One description per project page, written from the project PDFs and photos.

---

## Soleil
*Hospitality · Opulent waterside getaway*

A historic river voyage reimagined for today's traveller. Soleil draws on the golden sands and deep blues of the Nile, pairing warm marble, soft golden light and sculpted timber with quiet Pharaonic references: lotus motifs, papyrus-inspired columns and hand-painted details. Suites open onto uninterrupted river views, the dining room is framed by fluted columns and greenery, and a teak-decked rooftop and sundeck invite guests to slow down. The result is opulent but calm, a deep sense of tranquillity carried from cabin to deck.

---

## Lumière
*Hospitality · A luxe voyage*

Lumière brings luxury and relaxation together on the water. The interiors are a layered blend of polished wood, soft velvet and marble, warmed by ambient light and lifted by bold touches of green. Sculpted palm screens, patterned terrazzo floors and handcrafted furniture give each space its own character. The suites, in a soft, water-inspired palette, feel like private sanctuaries with the river right outside the window. From the dining room to the outdoor deck, every space is designed for slow mornings, quiet afternoons and evenings beneath the Nile sky.

## Le Fayan Suites
*Hospitality · Graceful seaside expedition*

A project that combines nature with sophistication. Le Fayan Suites is designed around warmth and light: honey-toned woods, marble accents and sweeping curved forms run from the sculptural staircase to the lobby bar, where soft lighting and lush greenery create a calm social space. Arched windows frame the passing landscape, cabins mix textured fabrics with patterned screens and artwork, and the sundeck, with its pool and shaded loungers, offers a serene open-air retreat. Every corner balances elegance with ease.

## Osiria Dahbeya
*Hospitality · A cultural experience*

Osiria Dahbeya is a journey rooted in place. Warm woods, natural rattan and latticed timber screens nod to traditional Egyptian craft, while soft pinks, sage greens and handwoven textures keep the mood light and welcoming. The reception and lounges are designed for effortless hospitality, and the Royal Exclusive Suite is bathed in natural sunlight with bespoke furnishings and curated details. Open, airy sundecks frame the endless horizon, with greenery that carries the interior living out into the open air.

---

## Al Batros Dahabeya
*Hospitality · A sanctuary at sea*

Al Batros is designed as a holistic retreat on the water, focused on wellbeing, comfort and understated elegance. A soft palette of creams, natural stone and refined gold accents sets a calm, luxurious tone, while sculptural columns, arched doorways and subtle Pharaonic artwork give the spaces a timeless character. Spaces flow from tranquil spa and fitness areas to elegant lounges, and large panoramic windows fill every room with natural light and uninterrupted views. It's a journey of rest, rejuvenation and mindful living.

## Paradise Katameya
*Residential · Blending sculptural design with nature*

A contemporary family home built around a dramatic double-height living space. Warm timber, soft stone and veined grey marble form a calm, natural palette, brought to life by sculptural pieces: a raw stone fire table, a spiralling staircase and bold, organic lighting. Interiors open generously to the garden and pool, with greenery and water drawn inside through tall glazing. Every room is defined by clean lines and seamless transitions, creating a home that feels both striking and deeply relaxed.

## Hacienda White
*Residential · A quiet balance of texture, light and proportion*

Hacienda White is a home of soft curves and natural warmth. Oak, carved timber and woven cane ceilings sit against creamy plaster walls, arched niches and gently rounded stairs, while sheer curtains filter the light into a calm, golden glow. Muted neutrals are lifted by olive stone, sage accents and deep blue tiles, giving each space a relaxed Mediterranean character. From the bedroom suites to the open terrace and shaded garden pavilion, it's a home made for slow, sunlit living.

## FA·NG
*Residential · Sun-soaked, relaxed living · New Giza*

A villa in New Giza designed for sun-soaked, relaxed living. Outside, a mosaic-tiled pool, palms and arched windows set a resort-like mood. Inside, rich marble is softened by symmetry and balanced proportions. Glass, stone and brass create a layered dialogue of colour, texture and pattern, from black-framed marble bathrooms to a blush bedroom wrapped in hand-painted blossom wallpaper. The result is elegant and light-filled, with a quiet sense of glamour in every detail.

---

## Creek Town
*Residential · Layered with character*

Creek Town is a home layered with character, where timeless details meet a modern aesthetic. Coffered ceilings, graceful arches and crystal chandeliers set a classic backdrop, while bold geometric marble floors, art-deco inspired doors and a deep red powder room add a confident contemporary edge. Collected artwork, framed portraits and a hand-painted floral screen bring personality to every room, creating an interior that feels elegant, expressive and entirely its own.
